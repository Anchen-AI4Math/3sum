TITLE
Deterministic Integer 3SUM in O(n^1.999017) Time via a Sharper Binomial-Tail Analysis

ABSTRACT
We refine the analysis of the deterministic integer 3SUM algorithm of Alman and Vassilevska Williams. Within their framework of thin matrix products and reductions through Exact Triangle, we obtain a worst-case running time of O(n^1.999017) for polynomially bounded integer inputs on a word RAM with O(log n)-bit words. The refinement retains the binomial tail in the preprocessing cost instead of replacing it immediately by a uniform geometric bound. An entropy estimate improves the preprocessing saving exponent while preserving the query algorithm. Explicit rational parameters give a 3SUM exponent of 1.999016414375 + o(1), leaving a positive margin below the stated fixed exponent. Retuning the previous analysis alone gives O(n^1.999073). We account for encoding, queries, prime selection, instance construction, witness scanning, and parameter rounding. The numerical inequalities admit exact rational interval certificates. This is an analysis refinement of an existing algorithm and its reductions; no complete Lean formalization of the improved bounds, global optimality, or priority claim is made.
